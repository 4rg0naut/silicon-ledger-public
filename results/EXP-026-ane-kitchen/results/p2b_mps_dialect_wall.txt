Two additions to the plugin route:
  1. bench/mlir_bytecode.py `rewrite` — rewrites the private dialects' attribute/type
     ENTRIES in sections 2/3 of the bytecode into textual placeholders (loc(unknown)/i64),
     re-emitting the section framing. Private VALUES are stubbed; builtin-encoded values
     survive; the op graph is recovered.
  2. The plugin keeps the version + resource interfaces (above).
Then: mlir-opt --load-dialect-plugin=<plugin.dylib> --allow-unregistered-dialect <rw>
prints readable IR.

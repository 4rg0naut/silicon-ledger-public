FINDINGS

  1. **Apple's on-device model uses the Neural Engine** — measured with the calibrated
     exclusive lens, no xctrace. The ANE-exclusive counter `SOC-NI9 ANEXL U` reads **0 in
     both idle windows and 10,862 during the fm window** (ANE UP corroborates: 0/25 -> 10,839).
     This upgrades EXP-002's xctrace-based observation ("20 ane-hw-intervals ~184 ms") to a
     repeatable, unprivileged measurement on the same box.

  2. **The `Fast-Die CE` compute-utilization histogram is dead on M5 Max**: the channel
     `PMP0/Fast-Die CE/ANE0` (present in the subscription) reads **0 in every window** —
     idle, confirmed-ANE (EXP-027 promotion matrix), GPU, and this fm window. This is the
     channel `tmc/aneperf` derives `ane_utilization_pct` from (LANDSCAPE-073), so an
     ANE-utilisation number built on it cannot work on this machine; ANEXL is the working
     signal. (Caveat: one chip/OS build; other chips may deliver the histogram.)

  3. **Lane mix**: FM is ANE-active and does not engage the GPU above the idle baseline
     (GPU Energy is *not* higher during the fm window; UT shows only Perf_State_1, no
     tensor/PS13 engagement). The ANE L0/L1 DCS+AF lanes move during FM but also in idle2
     (non-exclusive) — consistent with EXP-027; do not use them as the ANE signal.

NOTES / CAVEATS
  - Window **durations differ** (idle 12 s vs the fm window); the energy columns are totals
    and are NOT directly comparable across rows — only the zero-vs-nonzero structure is.
    `ANEXL U` is zero regardless of duration, which is the load-bearing comparison.
  - 25 calls x ~184 ms ANE (EXP-002) ~ 4.6 s of ANE work in the window -> a low duty cycle;
    the point is presence/absence and lane identity, not a duty estimate.
  - `Energy Model/ANE0` and CPU Energy stay 0 (gated, known). No mJ claim is made here.
  - Per-op / per-asset attribution is NOT available: `fm` is an opaque server and the
    guardrails' share of the ~150 ms warm cost remains unisolated (EXP-002 unknown).

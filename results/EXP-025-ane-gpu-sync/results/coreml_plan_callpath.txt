ADDRESS-SPACE CORRECTION (why the first attempt was wrong)
----------------------------------------------------------
`ipsw dyld disass --vaddr <runtime IMP>` and `ipsw dyld a2s` do NOT share the runtime address
space: a first decompile of 0x191f7ab84 returned BLAS code (cblas_dsymm), and our own runtime dump
of the SAME address begins with a prologue (`hint #27; sub sp,sp,#80; stp ...`). The authoritative
bytes are therefore the ones read from the loaded image at runtime; ipsw remains useful for
extraction and symbol names but its --vaddr space must be mapped first.

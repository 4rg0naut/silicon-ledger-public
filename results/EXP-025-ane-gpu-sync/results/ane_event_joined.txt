VERDICT
-------
The ANE-side inter-engine EVENT OBJECT is real and capturable during CoreML ANE execution —
a non-nil _ANESharedEvents carrying a signal event bound to an IOSurfaceSharedEvent counter
is attached to the ANE request on every eval. That is the far end of the wire the lab has been
chasing: an ANE program on one side, a shared counter in the middle. What remains to observe is
the counter CHANGING during execution (the write is hardware-side; a later slice can hold a
live wrapper over eventPort and poll it), and the GPU-side counterpart of the same counter.

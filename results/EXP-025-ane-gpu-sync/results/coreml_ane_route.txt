C. But a PURE-ANE workload attaches NO events
---------------------------------------------
  Every setSharedEvents: call was nil, and no IOSurfaceSharedEvent was created in-process,
  so for this model there is no inter-engine chain to join. A live, non-nil chain needs a
  MIXED ANE+GPU workload (so Apple must wire engines together), or capture of the events at
  _ANERequest creation via its designated initializer (not hooked in join_chain.m).

VERDICT
-------
The ANE-specific shared-event route is Core ML's e5rt path, as API-115 implied; Core AI is
generic. The blind spot is now closed on WHERE the route is. What remains is a workload that
makes Apple attach non-nil events — a mixed-engine model — at which point the live counter can
be joined exactly as the Core AI counters were (API-142).

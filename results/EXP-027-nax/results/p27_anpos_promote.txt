VERDICT

  1. **SOC-NI9 "ANEXL U" — PROMOTED.** Zero in every non-ANE window (idle ×2, GPU ×2, CPU ×1)
     and ≈19.6k in both ANE windows (spread <0.2%). It is a **lane-exclusive, unprivileged ANE
     activity counter** on M5 Max. Registers as a metric: non-zero ⇒ ANE lane was active in the
     window; zero ⇒ not.
  2. **SOC-NI8 "ANE UP" — PROMOTED with a declared residual.** ≈18.4k ANE vs 13 in each GPU
     window (~0.07%), 0 at idle/CPU → 1417× separation. Use ANEXL as the primary exclusive lens;
     ANE UP as corroboration.
  3. **PMP0 DCS/AF ANE L0/L1 RD+WR — CONFIRMED NON-EXCLUSIVE.** 35.3k ANE vs 21.7k GPU (1.6×):
     traffic is higher during ANE work but also present in the GPU window. Keep as an ANE-lane
     *indicator*, never a discriminator. (This is exactly the 2026-10-08 caveat, now quantified.)
  4. **ANE-DCS-BW floor residency — NOT a discriminator in this session.** idle ≈299M > ANE ≈204M;
     the F/voltage-floor residency is saturated by non-ANE DCS activity (display/fabric) and does
     not track the ANE lane. Do not use the floor lens as an ANE signal under this sampling.

CONSEQUENCES

  - The EXP-022/EXP-025/EXP-026/K6b "ANE 0" routings stand as **true negatives**: the exclusive
    lens (ANEXL) is proven to light up (≈19.6k) under real ANE work and stay at exactly 0
    otherwise, including during GPU-lane execution of the same model.
  - ANEXL is the unprivileged answer to "did the NE lane run?" that this lab has hunted since
    EXP-022; it does not require sudo, does not require the Energy Model rail (gated), and is
    disjoint from the GPU-side UT Perf_State_13 lens.
  - enginemon should expose ANEXL/ANE-UP as a first-class field (currently a raw --all channel).

CAVEATS
  - One machine/OS build (26A434); the ANE arm is the JIT route on a single full-ANE-region
    graph (the h17g AOT bundle no longer loads on h17c — falls back to JIT; lane placement is
    baked by the model author, and 76.5 ms matches the ANE class).
  - CPU window n=1 (control); idle/GPU n=2. ANEXL's zero in all five non-ANE windows and its
    sub-0.2% ANE-window spread is the strength of the claim; a future capture can widen n.
  - Window-level attribution, not per-eval.

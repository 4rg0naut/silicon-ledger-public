WX-3 — WRITE-SIDE mpsgraphtool round-trip through our MLIR-bytecode reader (2026-10-08)
=====================================================================================
Machine: Mac17,14 M5 Max (h17c), macOS 27.0.1 (26A434), user-run, unprivileged.

WHY: SU decoded Apple's WRITE artifacts (coreai-cache plan packages) produced by the
runtime. Until now nothing showed a PUBLIC tool writing the same format, and nothing
showed whether the placement grammar is reachable without the runtime. mpsgraphtool
(/usr/bin, "usage: mpsgraphtool convert -coremlpackage … -specializeForDevice",
the same tool Apple's LLVM RFC promises) closes both questions.

Commands (verbatim):
  mpsgraphtool convert -coremlpackage models/minilm128.mlpackage \
      -path /tmp/mpsbc -packageName minilm128
  -> minilm128.mpsgraphpackage/{reflection.fb,manifest.plist,
     original_model_0.mpsgraph,resources.bin}

  mpsgraphtool convert -coremlpackage models/minilm128.mlpackage \
      -path /tmp/mpsbc -packageName minilm128spec -specializeForDevice
  -> minilm128spec.mpsgraphpackage/{..., original_model_0.mpsgraph,
     specialized_model_1.mpsgraph}   # SAME naming as coreai-cache (SU finding 7)
